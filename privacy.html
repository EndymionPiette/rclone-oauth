Privacy policy

This policy describes the personal Google Drive OAuth connection used with Rclone for MathDocs.

Purpose and intended users

The OAuth application is intended only for the account owner's personal use. It allows the owner to authorize the open-source Rclone program to access their own Google Drive from their own devices. It is not a public web application or a service offered to other users.

Google Drive data accessed

After the account owner grants OAuth permission, Rclone may read file names, metadata and content, and may create, update, move or delete files as allowed by the Google Drive scopes granted and by the commands executed by the owner. Although the current workflow focuses on a MathDocs folder, the OAuth permission may cover a broader portion of the owner's Drive.

How data is used

Data is accessed by Rclone to browse, mount, download, upload, synchronize or otherwise manage the owner's files as directed by the owner. This informational website does not connect to Google Drive or receive copies of those files.

Storage and sharing

Rclone stores its OAuth credentials locally in the user's Rclone configuration, subject to the device's security settings. Files and any local caches are stored on the owner's devices or in the cloud locations the owner configures. This website does not maintain a separate database or server receiving Drive files, and the operator does not sell Drive data or provide it to advertisers.

Retention and revoking access

The owner manages the retention of files, local caches and Rclone configuration. Google OAuth authorization can be revoked from the owner's Google Account connections; local Rclone credentials can also be removed from the local configuration.

Website hosting

These static pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They use no embedded analytics, tracking scripts, contact forms or application cookies. GitHub may process ordinary website access information under its own privacy statement.

Contact

For questions about this personal OAuth integration, use the support contact displayed on its Google OAuth consent screen.
